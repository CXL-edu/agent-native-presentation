# Asset Contract

The example deck uses local SVG assets. `assets/agent-loop.svg` is the static visual and `assets/agent-loop-animated.svg` is the motion variant.

- Keep image paths local and include them in `GROUNDING.md` when they carry evidence.
- Use `contain` for diagrams and UI captures unless a deliberate crop is stated.
- Preserve alt text, caption, and source labels.
- Do not use images to fill empty space or hide weak slide structure.
